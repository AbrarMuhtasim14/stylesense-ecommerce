STYLESENSE COMPANY POLICY DOCUMENT
====================================

SECTION 1: RETURN POLICY
-------------------------
StyleSense accepts returns within 30 calendar days of the delivery date. Items must be in their original, unworn condition with all tags attached and original packaging intact. Items that show signs of wear, washing, alteration, or damage will not be accepted for return.

Intimate wear, swimwear, and accessories marked as "final sale" are non-refundable under any circumstances. Footwear returns must include the original shoe box in undamaged condition.

To initiate a return, customers must contact our Style Assistant chatbot or email support@stylesense.com with their order number and reason for return. A return authorization will be issued within 24 hours of the request.

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the return is due to a defect or incorrect item received. StyleSense provides free return shipping for defective or wrong items.

SECTION 2: REFUND POLICY
-------------------------
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. The refund will be issued to the original payment method used during purchase.

For defective items, a full refund including original shipping charges will be provided. For change-of-mind returns, only the item price will be refunded; original shipping costs are non-refundable.

If an item arrives damaged during transit, customers must report it within 48 hours of delivery with photographic evidence. Late damage reports may not be eligible for refund.

Partial refunds may be issued for items returned in less-than-original condition at StyleSense's discretion.

SECTION 3: EXCHANGE POLICY
---------------------------
Exchanges are available for different sizes or colors of the same item, subject to availability. Exchange requests must be made within 30 days of delivery.

For exchanges, StyleSense provides free shipping for the replacement item. If the exchanged item has a price difference, the customer will be charged or refunded the difference accordingly.

Size exchanges are processed on a priority basis and typically ship within 2-3 business days of receiving the original item.

SECTION 4: SHIPPING POLICY
----------------------------
Standard shipping within Bangladesh is free on all orders above ৳1,500. Orders below ৳1,500 incur a flat shipping fee of ৳100.

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days within Dhaka and 5-7 business days for other cities in Bangladesh.

Express shipping is available at ৳250 for delivery within 1-2 business days (Dhaka only).

International shipping is currently not available. All orders are shipped with tracking numbers provided via email and SMS.

SECTION 5: ORDER CANCELLATION POLICY
--------------------------------------
Orders can be cancelled free of charge while they are in "processing" status, before they are shipped. Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be cancelled and must go through the return process.

Cancellation requests are processed instantly through our Style Assistant chatbot. Refunds for cancelled orders are issued within 2-3 business days.

If an order has already been packed but not yet shipped, cancellation may take up to 24 hours to process.

SECTION 6: PRODUCT QUALITY GUARANTEE
--------------------------------------
StyleSense guarantees the quality of all products sold on our platform. If a product has a manufacturing defect, we offer a full replacement or refund within 60 days of purchase.

Quality issues include: stitching defects, fabric tears, color bleeding, broken zippers/buttons, and incorrect sizing from the manufacturer.

Normal wear and tear, damage from improper care (e.g., machine washing a dry-clean-only item), and cosmetic preferences (e.g., color looks different on screen) are not covered under our quality guarantee.

SECTION 7: SIZE GUIDE
-----------------------
StyleSense provides size charts on each product page. Sizing may vary between brands and product categories.

For tops (Men): S (36-38"), M (38-40"), L (40-42"), XL (42-44"), XXL (44-46")
For tops (Women): XS (30-32"), S (32-34"), M (34-36"), L (36-38"), XL (38-40")
For bottoms: Please refer to waist and inseam measurements on individual product pages.
For footwear: We recommend ordering your regular size. If between sizes, size up for a more comfortable fit.

If a customer receives an item that doesn't match the size chart measurements, they are eligible for a free exchange.

SECTION 8: PRIVACY AND DATA
-----------------------------
StyleSense respects customer privacy. We collect only necessary information for order processing and delivery. Customer data is never shared with third parties for marketing purposes without explicit consent.

Payment information is processed through secure, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. StyleSense does not store credit card numbers on our servers.

Customers can request deletion of their account and associated data by contacting support@stylesense.com.

SECTION 9: CUSTOMER SERVICE HOURS
-----------------------------------
Our AI Style Assistant is available 24/7 for product inquiries, order tracking, and automated processes like cancellations and return initiation.

Human support agents are available Sunday through Thursday, 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM (Bangladesh Standard Time) for escalated issues.

Response time for email inquiries: within 24 hours on business days.
